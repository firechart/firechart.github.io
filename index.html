FHIR® data you can read

FireChart makes tools for FHIR® data that run in the browser. They read a FHIR® server or a Bundle directly, keep no data of their own and fit into your application with one script.

FireChart Viewer Source on GitHub

Viewer

Browse any FHIR® server: resource types, lists with FHIR® search, a record with its references, the summary of a patient. SMART on FHIR sign-in.

Try it

Bundle Visualizer

A FHIR® Bundle drawn as the graph of the references between its resources. Paste it, open a file or read a search with its related resources.

Try it

Embed it

One ES module puts the Viewer or the Visualizer into your page in a frame of its own. Plain HTML, React, Angular, Vue, or a Blazor component.
